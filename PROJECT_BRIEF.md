# Squab Social Creator

## Purpose

Create a repeatable, zero-cost, human-approved social content workflow for Squab Storage Bridgwater. The website is the control centre for both nine-post batches and quick reactive one-off posts.

## Agreed workflow

1. Choose a professionally balanced campaign style, then set the seasonal brief and local focus.
2. Copy the complete prompt generated by the website into ordinary ChatGPT.
3. Copy ChatGPT's single structured JSON response back into the website.
4. Import all content in one action.
5. Assign reusable Squab photographs, then review the artwork, captions, dates, times, CTAs, output formats and QA results.
6. Download one publishing pack containing the artwork, captions, schedule, reusable JSON, contact sheet and instructions.
7. Human-review and schedule the posts through Meta Business Suite.

## Deliberate boundaries

- The public static website does not call the OpenAI API.
- ChatGPT does not send content directly to the website.
- The website does not store an API key or Meta access token.
- Facebook and Instagram publishing remains a human-approved action in Meta Business Suite.
- These boundaries keep the workflow free and avoid exposing credentials in a public website.

## Brand rules

- Tone: warm, calm, empathetic, human and professional, with occasional gentle wit.
- Messaging: stress reduction, clear pricing, flexibility, peace of mind and local Bridgwater relevance.
- Sales style: transparent and informative. No aggressive selling, fake urgency or unsupported claims.
- Primary colours: `#f4941e`, `#e95822`, `#fabd75`, `#fddfb7`, `#000000`, `#313131`, `#d2d2d2` and `#ffffff`.
- Typography: Lato Black for main titles, Lato Bold for supporting headings and Lato Regular for body copy.
- Primary social artwork size: 1080 x 1350 pixels, with square and LinkedIn output options.
- Imagery must visibly show the reason storage is useful. Avoid spotless, empty or already perfectly decluttered rooms.
- Use the genuine Squab logo only. Do not redraw, distort, recolour, shadow or place it directly over photography.

## Required product features

- Guided five-step workflow
- Nine-post batch and one-off modes
- Fifteen campaign-direction presets covering household, business, brand, local community and timely content
- Verified Squab facts supplied to the content prompt to reduce unsupported claims
- Complete ChatGPT prompt generation
- Structured JSON import and validation, including legacy template compatibility
- Twenty-four artwork templates across twelve distinct design families
- Template recommendations and automatic visual-variety rules
- Official logo rendering in a controlled solid logo zone
- Persistent reusable photograph library with automatic assignment
- Photograph replacement and artwork editing
- Caption, CTA, contact detail, date, time and publication-status editing
- Portrait, square and LinkedIn export choices
- Per-post and batch QA
- Saved campaign library with reopen, duplicate and status controls
- Complete ZIP publishing-pack export with PNG artwork
- Contact-sheet PNG export
- Clear Meta Business Suite handoff

## Definition of done

The workflow is complete when a user can start with one brief and finish with a ZIP containing correctly named PNGs, matching captions, a posting schedule, the reusable batch JSON, a contact sheet and simple publishing instructions, without manually re-entering each post. Nothing is published until a human has reviewed and approved it.
