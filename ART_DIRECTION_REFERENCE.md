# Approved art-direction reference

The attached/approved concept board from 27 Aug 2026 is the visual benchmark for the next renderer iteration.

## Required finished direction

The production templates must visually track the approved concept board, including:

- cinematic, image-led compositions rather than empty graphic panels
- bold oversized Lato typography with aggressive cropping and hierarchy
- orange marker strokes and hand-drawn/tactile accents used as energy, not decoration
- real foreground/background interaction
- collage, torn-paper and pinned-note effects where appropriate
- believable customer situations and strong focal subjects
- high-contrast black/orange/white treatment with photography doing most of the visual work
- asymmetry, tension and directional movement
- large single-minded headlines and concise supporting copy
- playful, surprising art direction where the content allows it
- strong local and human storytelling where relevant

## Rejection criteria

Reject any implementation that becomes:

- flat corporate blocks
- presentation-slide layouts
- dashboard or SaaS UI aesthetics
- large dead areas with tiny copy
- generic dark rectangles labelled as photo placeholders
- decorative geometry standing in for photography
- weak hierarchy or timid type scale
- repetitive two-column or card-grid structures
- generic AI-looking compositions

## Implementation milestone

Do not expand to 36-48 executions yet.

First build six production-quality hero templates that visibly match the approved board in spirit and energy:

- Bold Impact
- Real People
- Transformation
- Lifestyle Freedom
- Business & Workspace
- Creative / Tactile

These six must be reviewed in-app with real photography before the remaining territories or variants are expanded.

The live `main` branch remains out of scope until explicit human approval.
